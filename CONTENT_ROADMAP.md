# Content Roadmap

Every zone follows the same shape: 4–6 short levels (6–8 challenges each, about 3 minutes) and a checkpoint that mixes all of them. Content is general education. **All exercise and safety content needs review by a qualified fitness professional (e.g. a certified personal trainer or physical therapist) before public release.**

## Zone 1 · Front Door — ✅ playable

| Level | Teaches | Challenge types |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First Visit | What to bring, sensible order of a visit, the spotlight effect, realistic first session, first-day mistakes | choice, sort, sequence, spot |
| Read the Floor | The five gym zones, why machines are beginner-friendly, where to warm up | hotspot (floor plan), match, choice, sort |
| Ask Like a Regular | Who to ask, when to ask, "work in", staff vs. paid training | choice, match, sequence, sort |
| House Rules | Rerack, wipe down, sharing, filming etiquette, barbell cleanup order | choice, spot, sort, sequence |
| Know the Kit | Common equipment, the stack pin vs. seat pin, rack safety arms, treadmill safety key | picture, hotspot ×3, match |
| Checkpoint | Mixed review of everything above | all 7 types |

## Zone 2 · Body Basics — planned

- Major muscle groups (a tap-the-body hotspot on a neutral body diagram)
- Movement patterns: squat, hinge, push, pull, carry (sorting exercises by pattern)
- Sets, reps and rest: reading "3 × 10, 60s rest"
- Reading an exercise card or app instruction
- Checkpoint

## Zone 3 · Equipment Floor — planned

- Cardio machines: start, stop, incline and safety
- Dumbbells: picking a starting weight by feel (controlled reps with a couple left in the tank), not by number
- Machine setup: seat height, range-of-motion settings, aligning joints with pivot points
- Cables: pulley height, attachments, stance
- Safe starting practices
- Checkpoint

## Zone 4 · First Moves — planned (needs expert review first)

- Squat pattern (goblet squat / box squat), push (incline press, push-up progressions), pull (cable row, lat pulldown), hinge (hip hinge drill, kettlebell deadlift)
- Beginner alternatives for each
- **Effort vs. pain**: what normal muscle effort feels like vs. warning signs
- New challenge type planned: **"Spot the form fix"** (compare two positions)

## Zone 5 · Build a Session — planned (needs expert review first)

- Warm-ups, choosing 3–5 exercises, order (bigger movements first), rest periods, simple progression, recovery and sleep basics
- New challenge type planned: **"Build the session"** (assemble a balanced beginner session from cards, with feedback on balance and order rather than volume)

## Zone 6 · Solo Visit — planned

- Branching real-floor scenarios: busy gym, equipment taken, forgot the plan, someone offers unsolicited advice
- Choosing an approachable routine and a printable/saveable "first visit card"
- Final review

## Content standards

- Every challenge teaches one concept. The `explain` field says why, not just what.
- Every wrong option in a multiple-choice question has its own `why`. (Enforced by a test.)
- No body-image language, no weight-loss promises, no "push through it".
- Pain guidance always says to stop and, if it persists, consult a qualified health professional.
- Never prescribe specific loads, volumes or programs to an individual.
